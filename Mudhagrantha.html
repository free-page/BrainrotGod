🔥 मूढ़ग्रन्थः 🔥

१: शौचं परमः नियमः

(Śaucaṁ paramaḥ niyamaḥ)

Hygiene is the highest rule.

अ) स्वगन्धानाज्ञानं महापातकम्।

(A) Svagandhānājñānaṁ mahāpātakam.

Being unaware of one’s own odor is a great sin.

ब) अशुचितभोज्यनिर्मातुं भोक्तुञ्च पापम्।

(B) Aśucitabhojyanirmātuṁ bhoktuñca pāpam.

It is a sin to prepare and eat unclean food.

क) मूढेश्वरपूजायां शुद्धो भवेत्, हस्तपाद्मुखप्रक्षालनमावश्यकम्।

(C) Mūḍheśvarapūjāyāṁ śuddho bhavet, hastapādmukhaprakṣālanamāvaśyakam.

Before worshipping Mūḍheśvara, one must be clean; washing hands, feet, and face is necessary.

२: सहिष्णुता धर्मः

(Sahiṣṇutā dharmaḥ)

Tolerance is a virtue.

संसारपरिवर्तनं स्वीकर्तव्यं, किन्तु मूढनियमलङ्घनं मा।

(Saṁsāraparivartanaṁ svīkartavyaṁ, kintu mūḍhaniyamalaṅghanaṁ mā.)

One must accept worldly changes, but breaking Mūḍha’s laws is not allowed.

धर्मभाषावर्णलिङ्गधनहेतोर्द्वेषो मा।

(Dharmabhāṣāvarṇaliṅgadhanahetordveṣo mā.)

Hate due to religion, language, caste, gender, or wealth is forbidden.
